How to use BGB Command
1
Set up once — Settings
Go to ⚙ Settings and enter your Anthropic API key, Google Sheets ID, and alliance name. Upload your alliance logo to personalize the app for your team.
API key from console.anthropic.com — needed for AI scanning. Stored only in your browser.
1
Keep your roster updated — Roster tab
Click ↻ Refresh Roster before each BGB event to pull the latest Main Car CP from your Google Sheets. Use Update Color Coding to see who has been absent (red) or hasn't played recently (green).
Before each BGB event
2
Scan participant screenshots — Register
Take screenshots of the BGB Participants page for both Team A and Team B. Upload all screenshots, then tap Scan. The AI will match players to your roster automatically.
After scanning, use Review Results to verify each screenshot — you can delete wrong matches or add missing players manually.
3
Generate team formation — Teams
Tap Generate Teams → to automatically sort all players by Main Car CP and form the 10 teams + Alloy squad. Use the 📋 Copy buttons to paste the formation into your game chat.
Team 7–10 = top 8 players (snake draft). Alloy = top 4, dispatched after 10 minutes. Team 1–6 = remaining players.
After the battle
4
Record attendance — Attendance
Take ~9 screenshots of the post-battle results. Upload them and tap Scan. The AI checks who attended against your registered list and marks everyone as Present or Absent.
Tap any player row to manually toggle their attendance if the AI made a mistake.
Tip: Export a JSON backup regularly via Settings → Save / Load Data. Share the JSON with teammates so everyone sees the same color coding on the roster.
